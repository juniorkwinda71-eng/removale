What is Find My?
Apple's built-in tracking network

Find My is Apple's official system for locating your iPhone, iPad, Mac, AirPods, and Apple Watch. It uses GPS, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Apple's massive crowdsourced network of 2 billion+ devices to pinpoint your phone — even when it's offline.

Enable Find My iPhone
First-time setup on your device
Settings › [Your Name] › Find My › Find My iPhone
1

Open Settings on your iPhone.

2

Tap your name at the top (Apple ID).

3

Tap Find My.

4

Tap Find My iPhone and toggle it ON.

5

Also enable Enable Offline Finding — this lets your iPhone be found even without internet.

6

Enable Send Last Location — this sends GPS data to Apple when battery is critically low.

💡 Tip: You must be signed in with your Apple ID for Find My to work. Without iCloud sign-in, tracking is not possible.
📍
Location Services
Make sure location is ON
Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services
Location Services
Master switch — must be ON
Find My → While Using
Set to "Always" for best results
✅ Recommended: Set Find My location access to Always so it can report location even when the app isn't open.
☁️
Track via iCloud.com
From any browser, anywhere
1

Open any browser and go to icloud.com/find

2

Sign in with your Apple ID and password.

3

Complete two-factor authentication if prompted.

4

Click "All Devices" at the top of the map.

5

Select your iPhone from the list — it will show on the map.

6

You'll see options: Play Sound, Lost Mode, Erase iPhone.

💡 Tip: iCloud.com works from Windows, Android, Chromebook — any device with a browser. This is the best method when you don't have another Apple device.
📊
Reading the Map
Understanding what you see
🟢

Green dot — iPhone is online and location is live.

⚫

Grey dot — iPhone is offline. Shows last known location with a timestamp.

🔵

Blue circle — Approximate location radius. Larger = less precise.

⚠️ Note: If the phone is off or in airplane mode, you'll only see the last location before it went offline.

Using the Find My App
On another iPhone, iPad or Mac
1

Open the Find My app (green icon with radar waves).

2

Tap the Devices tab at the bottom.

3

Select your iPhone from the list.

4

The map shows your phone's location with an accuracy indicator.

5

Tap "Directions" to get turn-by-turn navigation to your phone.

🔊
Play a Sound
Find it nearby
1

Select your iPhone in Find My.

2

Tap "Play Sound" — your phone will beep loudly for 2 minutes.

3

The sound plays even if your phone is on silent.

💡 Great for: Finding your phone between couch cushions, under the bed, or in another room.
📡
Precision Finding
iPhone 11 and later

On iPhone 11 and later, Precision Finding uses Ultra Wideband (UWB) technology to give you directional arrows and distance to your iPhone — like a real-time compass pointing straight at it.

✅ How to use: Open Find My → Devices → tap your iPhone → tap "Find" → follow the on-screen arrow and distance meter.
👥
Share Your Location
With family or trusted contacts
1

Open Find My app → tap the People tab.

2

Tap "Share My Location".

3

Choose a contact from your list.

4

Select duration: 1 hour, Until end of day, or Indefinitely.

5

They'll receive a notification and can see your location on their Find My app.

💡 Two-way sharing: When you share, the other person can also share their location back with you.

Family Sharing
Track your whole family
Settings › [Your Name] › Family Sharing › Location Sharing
1

Set up Family Sharing in Settings with up to 5 family members.

2

Each member must accept the invitation sent to their Apple ID.

3

Once joined, all family members appear in the People tab of Find My.

4

See all family devices on one shared map in real time.

🚨 Lost Mode — When Your iPhone is Missing

Lost Mode locks your iPhone remotely, disables Apple Pay, and displays a custom message with a phone number on the lock screen. Your phone keeps reporting its location even in Lost Mode.

🔒
Enable Lost Mode
Lock and track immediately
1

Go to icloud.com/find or open Find My app on another device.

2

Select your iPhone from the device list.

3

Tap "Mark As Lost" → Activate.

4

Enter a phone number to display on the lock screen (your backup number or family member).

5

Type a message, e.g. "This iPhone is lost. Please call the number above."

6

Tap Activate. The iPhone is now locked and being tracked.

✅ What happens: Device locks immediately. Apple Pay disabled. Lock screen shows your message. Location updates continue. You get email notifications when found.
🔔
Location Notifications
Get alerted when it moves

In Lost Mode, you can enable location notifications — Apple sends you an email whenever your iPhone's location is detected or updated.

⚠️ Important: Do NOT attempt to personally confront someone who has your phone. Share the location with local authorities instead.

🗑️ Remote Erase — Last Resort Only

Remotely erasing your iPhone permanently deletes ALL data. Once erased, tracking is no longer possible. Only use this if you're certain the device is gone for good and you want to protect your personal data.

⚠️
How to Erase Remotely
Protect your data permanently
1

Go to icloud.com/find and sign in.

2

Select your iPhone.

3

Click "Erase iPhone".

4

Enter your Apple ID password to confirm.

5

The erase will begin next time the phone connects to the internet.

⚠️ Warning: After erasing, the phone can no longer be tracked. The device will still show in your account and is linked to your Apple ID — meaning no one else can activate it without your credentials (Activation Lock).
✅ Activation Lock: Even after a remote erase, the iPhone remains locked to your Apple ID. The thief cannot use or resell it without your password.

Pro Tips & Tricks
Get the most out of tracking
⚡

Enable "Send Last Location" in Find My settings. When battery hits 5%, it auto-sends GPS to Apple before dying.

🔋

iPhone 15 and later: Even when completely powered off, the phone emits a Bluetooth signal so Find My can still locate it.

🛡️

Never turn off Find My: In Settings → Screen Time, you can prevent Find My from being disabled. Great for kids' devices.

🔐

Use Stolen Device Protection: Settings → Face ID & Passcode → Stolen Device Protection. Adds a delay for sensitive actions when away from home.

📶

Offline tracking works: If your iPhone has no signal, other nearby Apple devices silently relay its location to Apple — privately and encrypted.

🌍

Works internationally: Find My works in every country as long as the phone has any connection or is near other Apple devices.

Troubleshooting
Common issues & fixes
❓

"Location Not Available" — Phone is off, in airplane mode, or battery is dead. Wait and check again.

❓

Location not updating — Make sure Location Services is set to "Always" for Find My. Toggle off/on in Settings.

❓

Device not showing in Find My — Confirm you're signed into the same Apple ID, and that Find My iPhone is toggled ON.

❓

Offline for hours — The phone may be in a dead zone. Check "Send Last Location" for the last GPS ping before it went offline.

❓

Location seems wrong — Indoor locations or areas with weak GPS may be imprecise. The blue accuracy circle shows the possible range.
